# Train Wreck Prompts

Prompts for *The ART of Avoiding a Train Wreck*, second edition, by Em Campbell-Pretty and Adrienne L. Wilson. Each file holds one prompt (the splitting file holds two), with a short note on what it is for and how to use it. Paste the prompt text into a new chat in your AI tool.

## These prompts will age

We wrote these prompts for the models we had in 2026. Models keep changing, and a prompt that works well on one can misbehave on the next. Earlier models did best with step-by-step instructions; newer ones do better when you give them the intent and let them work out the steps. If a prompt starts giving you odd results, ask your AI tool to rewrite it for the model you are using now, and tell it what the prompt is meant to achieve.

## Tell us

If a prompt stops working on your model, or you change one to make it work better, tell us at art@prettyagile.com. We read everything, though we can't reply to every message.

## The prompts

- [art-design-wrap.md](art-design-wrap.md): tests a chosen ART design with the Heaths' W.R.A.P. process; it never designs the ART itself.
- [epic-definition-critique.md](epic-definition-critique.md): checks a written Epic Hypothesis Statement and recommends adjustments.
- [feature-and-story-splitting.md](feature-and-story-splitting.md): two prompts, one to split a feature into stories and one to split a large story into smaller ones.
- [pi-objectives-coaching.md](pi-objectives-coaching.md): coaches a team on its draft PI objectives.
- [iteration-goals-first-draft.md](iteration-goals-first-draft.md): drafts two or three iteration goals from a team's iteration plan, for the team to review and edit.
- [sponsor-conversation-rehearsal.md](sponsor-conversation-rehearsal.md): rehearses a hard conversation with a sponsor, with the AI playing them and then debriefing you.
- [ip-iteration-pre-mortem.md](ip-iteration-pre-mortem.md): stress-tests an IP iteration plan by assuming it was wasted and working back to the causes.
- [problem-statement-interview.md](problem-statement-interview.md): interviews a team to turn a vague complaint into a specific problem statement (what, where, when and impact) for the Inspect and Adapt problem-solving workshop or Bubble Up.
- [feature-definition-interview.md](feature-definition-interview.md): runs a Feature Definition session, interviewing the group against its Definition of Ready (or the book's example list), pressure-testing the answers, and writing up the feature with a list of gaps, owners and dates.
- [feature-qa-review.md](feature-qa-review.md): checks a written feature for missing information and gaps in its business outcomes before prioritisation, and asks the questions that close them; it never rewrites the feature.
