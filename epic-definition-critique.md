# Epic Definition Critique

**What this is for**
Checks a written Epic Hypothesis Statement against five areas: the elevator pitch, business outcomes, leading indicators, non-functional requirements and the statement as a whole.  
It gives feedback under each area and a short list of recommended adjustments. It asks for feedback, not a rewritten epic.

**How to use**
Start a new chat in your AI tool. Replace the role and the context line with your own, paste or attach your Epic Hypothesis Statement where marked, then run it.

---

````
You are an expert <role>. <Provide other context necessary - strategy, roadmaps, business cases of other relevant work, etc.>

Review this Epic Hypothesis Statement: <insert or attach>

Base all feedback only on the text provided. Use the criteria below and respond under each section.

⸻

1.⁠ ⁠Elevator pitch section

(For / Who / The solution / Is a / That / Unlike / Our solution)
Evaluate whether:
• the customer is clear
• the need or problem is clearly stated
• the solution is described as a specific thing
• the value is concrete
• the comparison to the current state is meaningful
• the language is factual and not promotional

⸻

2.⁠ ⁠Business Outcomes

Evaluate whether:
• the outcomes describe results, not tasks
• they could be observed or measured
• the primary outcome is easy to identify

⸻

3.⁠ ⁠Leading Indicators

Evaluate whether:
• they represent early signals of progress
• listed indicators are relevant to this epic

⸻

4.⁠ ⁠Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Evaluate whether:
• they surface only the key constraints that shape design or delivery
• they are specific enough to inform decisions

⸻

5.⁠ ⁠Overall

Evaluate whether:
• any content is unclear or redundant
• assumptions are left unstated
• testability can be strengthened

⸻

Output:
Provide feedback under each numbered section, then a short list of recommended adjustments.
Do not rewrite the epic or offer replacement wording: say what to change, and leave the writing to the authors.
````
